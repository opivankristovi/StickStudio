    Welcome to StickStudio 👋

    1. Pick a layout in Page layout on the rightbelow the page. Use a preset, or set rows and panels per row. ClickTap a panel to split or merge it, or to change its width.
    2. Add a scene from the Scenes tab (the + button opens the library): drag it onto a panel, or click a panel and then the scene.tap a panel, then tap the scene.
    3. Add characters, objects and balloons the same way. Double-clickDouble-tap a balloon to type.
    4. Select something and use the toolbar at the top to change a character's pose and face, or to flip, rotate, resize, align it in its panel or change its layer. Colours and other settings are on the rightbelow the page.
    5. Add pages with the buttons under the page. Drag the page thumbnails to put them in a different order.Use the ◀ ▶ buttons to move a page.
    6. Export (top right) when you're done. Your work saves automatically, and Projects in the ⋯ menu lists all your cartoons.
