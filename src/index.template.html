    Welcome to StickStudio 👋

    1. Pick a layout on the right (Page layout). Use a preset, or set rows and panels per row. Click a panel to split or merge it, or to change its width.
    2. Drag a scene from the Scenes tab onto a panel.
    3. Drag characters, objects and balloons into the panels. Double-click a balloon to type.
    4. Add pages at the bottom. Drag the page thumbnails to put them in a different order.
    5. Export PDF when you're done. Your work saves automatically, and Projects lists all your cartoons.
